Gateway Connection Setup

Steps to link your Meta developer account to this portal.

1Register Business App

Go to the Meta Developers Portal, create a "Business" app type, and add the "WhatsApp" product product under your app.

2Vercel Environment Variables

In your Vercel project, configure these environment variables: GEMINI_API_KEY, WHATSAPP_TOKEN, VERIFY_TOKEN, and GOOGLE_SHEETS_URL.

3Save and Verify Webhook

Under Meta's WhatsApp -> Configuration, enter your Vercel URL (e.g. https://...vercel.app/api/webhook) and Verify Token, then subscribe to the messages field.
